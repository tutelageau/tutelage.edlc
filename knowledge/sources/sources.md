# Sources register

Every outside source the site or the cast relies on. A claim about the world reaches the site only when its source
is `verified`: opened, read, and the cited detail (author, year, title, page or clause) confirmed against the
original. `link-checked` means the paper's draft opened the page but the citation detail is not yet confirmed;
`from-memory` means nobody here has opened it. The `researcher` agent moves rows forward and records the date.
