## The gate

Content design's `does not hold` rested on P-59 alone. It is fixed, asserted by the check, and re-read. All three
design-test answers are "no", and all three turn on what does not yet exist: figures, a worked example on a real
unit, and sector evidence for schools. The release says so plainly and cannot supply them. Each "no" also produced
a fix this release could make (P-76, P-77, P-79). Their cases are carried to 0.4.0 intact.

Not proven by the bench: arrow-key scrolling of a focused table in a real browser (headless Chromium would not
scroll). The wrapper is focusable with a visible ring. A manual check is owed after deploy.
