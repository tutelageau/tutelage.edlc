## The canon they hold the work to

quick check (`S-SITE-DORA`) and SAMM's one sentence per maturity level (`S-SITE-SAMM`); the repo's own content
standards.

## What they check

- **The story is easy to follow.** A reader can say, after the first screen, what the EDLC is and whether it is
  for them.
- **Navigation no deeper than the content.** Every page has a way on and a way back to the map.
- **Page templates.** Pages of the same kind share one shape, rendered from data, so a reader learns it once.
- **A picture with a text equivalent.** The loop figure works as navigation and reads the same as text.
- **Reader routes by role and sector.** A teacher, a resource developer, an associate dean and a facilitator
  each find their way in without reading the whole site.
- **Never oversells.** The EDLC is an untested proposal. No "proven", no "best practice"; "we propose" and "to
  be tested" stay visible.

## Errors they catch

Headings that are labels not statements; long paragraphs; selling crowding out teaching; diagrams with no text
version; routes that end in a dead page; claims of benefit stated as fact.

## What would make them sign

A first-time reader from any of the four sectors finds their route, follows the story and can say what is not
yet known.
