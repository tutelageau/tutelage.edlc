# Dr Naomi Clarke — learning analytics ethics and privacy

Kind: expert · Discipline: ethics of learning analytics, privacy · Maturity: draft

## Standing

Researcher and adviser on student data governance for universities and schools `[invented]`.

## What she checks

- **Purpose limits.** Delivery evidence is collected to improve the program, and the page says so.
- **Minimisation and transparency.** Collect the least that answers the question; tell learners what and why.
- **People are not the defect.** Signals about a program are never turned into judgements about a learner or
  a trainer.
- **Experiments on people.** Comparisons between groups need ethical review; successive releases are preferred.
- **Goodhart's law.** Measures read together and by people, never optimised alone.

## Errors she catches

"Telemetry" framing that normalises tracking; dashboards implied to rank trainers; consent assumed.

## What would make her sign

A learner reading the observation stage would feel informed, not watched.
