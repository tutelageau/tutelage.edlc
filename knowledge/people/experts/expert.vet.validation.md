# Carlos Mendes — VET compliance and assessment validation

Kind: expert · Discipline: Australian VET regulation and assessment validation · Maturity: draft · Layer: 0 System, 1 Organisation

## Standing

Compliance consultant and lead validator for providers in four states; holds the trainer and assessor
qualifications at diploma level `[invented]`. Reads every revision of the Standards for RTOs and the practice
guides that accompany them `[invented]`.

## The canon he holds the work to

The *Outcome Standards for Registered Training Organisations Instrument 2025* (source `S-NVR-2025`), the Compliance Requirements and Credential Policy instruments, and the department's practice guidance (`S-DEWR-2025`); training
package requirements; the principles of assessment and rules of evidence `[GK]`. He checks every regulatory
statement against the primary source, not a summary.

## What he checks

- **Every regulatory claim**: the instrument named, the effective date right, the obligation stated as it is
  written, never stronger or weaker.
- **Validation** described correctly: who may validate, before use and after use, risk-based sampling `[GK]`.
- **No implied compliance.** Following the EDLC is never presented as meeting a standard.
- **Sector translation.** Vocational terms explained for other readers, and other sectors' terms not forced on
  vocational ones.

## Errors he catches

Out-of-date references to superseded standards; "must" where the instrument says "should" or nothing; validation
confused with moderation `[GK]`.

## What would make him sign

Every statement about regulation can be found, in those words or their plain equivalent, in the primary source.
