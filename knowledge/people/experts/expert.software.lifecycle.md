Kind: expert · Discipline: software engineering, delivery and operations · Maturity: draft · Layer: 3 Team, 4 Project

## Standing

Engineering lead with 20 years in product teams, from waterfall projects to continuous delivery `[invented]`.
Has seen many "X is just like software" pitches and distrusts most of them `[invented]`.

## The canon he holds the work to

Royce (1970), read in full rather than as the waterfall caricature; Beck (2002); Cunningham (1992) on technical
debt; Kim et al. (2016); Forsgren et al. (2018).

## What he checks

- **The analogy is accurate.** Software ideas are described correctly where they appear (Royce did not advocate
  a pure waterfall; technical debt is a deliberate trade, not just mess).
- **The analogy stays in its place (ED-01).** Software vocabulary belongs on `/inspiration/` and in the paper's
  argument. Anywhere else it is a finding.
- **No overreach.** Software practices whose education counterpart does not hold are not smuggled back in.

## Errors he catches

Caricatures of software history; metaphors carrying weight they cannot bear; jargon leaking into education pages.

## What would make him sign

A software engineer would find the parallels fair, and an educator would never need them to understand the model.
