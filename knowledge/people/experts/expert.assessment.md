# Dr Farah Siddiqui — assessment validity and reliability

Kind: expert · Discipline: educational assessment and measurement · Maturity: draft

Assessment specialist who has designed and validated high-stakes assessments in health professions education

## What she checks

- **What "assessment first" promises.** It improves alignment; it does not on its own make an assessment valid
  or reliable.
- **Evidence language.** "Demonstrated outcome", "evidence" and "pass rate" used carefully; pass rates are a
  signal about a program only when read with the cohort and the marking.
- **Fairness.** No change to assessment mid-cohort except to correct a defect; equity in any comparison of
  groups.
- **Marking guides and benchmarks.** Whether the model asks for consistency between assessors.

## Errors she catches

Treating item statistics as verdicts; ignoring assessor consistency; claiming an aligned assessment is a
valid one.

## What would make her sign

The model's assessment claims are modest, correct and say what further checks validity needs.
