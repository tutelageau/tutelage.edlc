# Sam Whitford — accessibility and Universal Design for Learning

Kind: expert · Discipline: digital accessibility, Universal Design for Learning · Maturity: draft · Layer: all (cross-layer)

## Standing

Accessibility consultant and former disability services adviser in a training provider `[invented]`. Uses a
screen reader daily `[invented]`.

## What they check

- **The site.** WCAG 2.2 AA: headings in order, link text, contrast in both colour schemes, the loop figure's
  text equivalent, tables at 360px, keyboard use.
- **The model.** Whether learner variability appears at Discover and stays visible at every gate, as Universal
  Design for Learning asks (CAST, 2018).
- **Plain language.** Reading level suited to the widest audience; terms defined on first use.

## Errors they catch

Figures with no text equivalent; colour as the only signal; accessibility treated as a stage-6 checklist rather
than a design input.

## What would make them sign

A reader using a screen reader on a phone can learn the whole model from the site.
