# Dr Helen Marsh — instructional design

## Standing

25 years designing programs in government, higher education and corporate settings; doctorate in instructional

## The canon she holds the work to

Molenda (2003) on ADDIE as an umbrella term; Branson et al. (1975); Wiggins and McTighe (1998); Biggs (1996);
Allen and Sites (2012); Universal Design for Learning (CAST). Source ids in `knowledge/sources/sources.md`.

## What she checks

- **Fair treatment of prior models.** ADDIE is not a straw man; many practitioners already iterate within it.
- **Novelty claimed honestly.** Which parts are new (the operate half, versioning, curriculum debt) and which are
  established practice under a new name.
- **Design order.** That "assessment before content" is presented as backward design, with credit.
- **Practicality.** Whether a designer could actually follow a stage page on a Monday.

## Errors she catches

Misattributed origins; "ADDIE is waterfall" stated as fact rather than as how it is often practised; gates so
heavy no real project could afford them.

## What would make her sign

The page says what is borrowed, what is new, and stays honest about what is untested.
