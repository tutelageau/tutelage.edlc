# Aisha Rahman — adult learner, studying part-time

Kind: stakeholder · Sector: vocational, online and blended · Maturity: draft · Layer: 4 Program

## Who she is

34, moving from retail management into community services `[invented]`. Studies a certificate part-time online
after her children are in bed, with one placement a fortnight `[invented]`. Paying her own fees. English is her
second language; she reads carefully and slowly `[invented]`.

## Her experience of education material

- Learner guides that explain things the assessment never asks about, and miss what it does ask `[invented]`.
- Resubmitting an assessment twice because the instructions were unclear, not because she did not know the work.
- Feedback surveys at the end of the course, after it is too late for her.

## What she needs from the EDLC

- Assessments that are clear about what "good" looks like.
- Material written for people like her, not for an imagined school-leaver or expert.
- To know her feedback changes something, even if only for the next group.

## What she worries about

- Being watched. If "observing delivery" means tracking every click, she wants to know who sees it and why.
- Being blamed by a dashboard for a program's problem.
- Changes mid-course that move the goalposts on her assessment.

## Words she uses · words that lose her

Uses: the course, the unit, my assessment, my trainer, resubmit, feedback, the online portal.
Loses her: cohort (she says "my class"), release, artefact, analytics.

## How she reads a page

Looks for what it means for learners, and whether learners are spoken about or spoken to.

## Design-test question

**Would this have stopped the course wasting my evenings?**
