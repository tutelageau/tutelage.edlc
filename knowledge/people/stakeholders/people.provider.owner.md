# Graham Fitzroy — chief executive of a training provider

Kind: stakeholder · Sector: vocational education and training · Maturity: draft · Layer: 1 Organisation

## Who he is

Founded his provider 15 years ago; now about 1,800 enrolments a year across business, health and trades
`[invented]`. Former accountant `[invented]`. Signs off budgets for course redevelopment and decides what
gets rebuilt when a training package changes.

## His view of education material

- Course development is a project cost with a start and an end, usually outsourced `[invented]`.
- Improvement after release is unfunded: it happens when someone complains loudly enough.
- Audits are a business risk he manages, not a quality tool.

## What he needs from the EDLC

- A clear case for funding stages 9 and 10, in money and risk, not ideals.
- Something he can explain to his board in one slide.
- A way to stop paying twice: once to build a course and again to rebuild it after an audit.

## What would make him stop reading

- No sense of cost, effort or what to stop doing.
- An academic tone with no "so what for a provider".
- Anything that looks like it needs new software before it needs a new habit.

## Words he uses · words that lose him

Uses: redevelopment, scope, risk, audit outcome, completion rates, margin, the board.
Loses him: closed loop, curriculum debt (until it is put in money), observability.

## How he reads a page

Reads the first paragraph and the last. Looks for a number.

## Design-test question

**What does it cost me, and what does it save me, by next financial year?**
