# Arjun Mehta — learning designer, central learning and teaching unit

Kind: stakeholder · Sector: higher education · Maturity: draft · Layer: 2 Department, 3 Team

## Who he is

Senior learning designer in a mid-sized metropolitan university's central learning and teaching unit, seven
years in the role `[invented]`. Trained as a secondary English teacher in Adelaide, then did a master's in
education `[invented]`. Supports course teams in two faculties, one at a time, on a project basis `[invented]`.

## His semester with education material

- Running workshops with course teams on learning outcomes and assessment, often three weeks before the
  approval deadline `[invented]`.
- Drawing course maps: which unit teaches and assesses which course learning outcome. Constructive alignment
  is the idea behind it (`S-BIGGS-1996`).
- Building online units in the learning management system, then handing them over to academics who change them
  without telling anyone `[invented]`.
- Seeing the same advice ignored in one faculty and taken up in another.

## What he needs from the EDLC

- A shared language he can use with academics who distrust "frameworks from the centre".
- A way to show a course team where a unit sits in the whole course before they redesign it.
- A record of design decisions that survives when the coordinator changes.

## What would make him stop reading

- A model that treats academics as content suppliers. They own their teaching, and he works by invitation.
- Anything that sounds like a learning management system vendor's pitch.
- Steps that ignore the approval calendar.

## Words he uses · words that lose him

Uses: course, unit, learning outcomes, course learning outcomes, alignment, assessment design, course map,
course team, approval, the LMS.
Loses him: release, deployment, defect, competency (in higher education he says "outcome").

## How he reads a page

Reads it twice: once for himself, once as the most sceptical academic he works with.

## Design-test question

**Could I walk a course team through this in one workshop and leave them wanting a second?**
