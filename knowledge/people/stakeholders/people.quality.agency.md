# Ruth Harlow — external audit lead

Kind: stakeholder · Sector: quality assurance · Maturity: draft

## Who she is

Leads audits of training providers for a quality body; previously a provider's academic director `[invented]`.
This sheet is a fictional auditor's view. It does not speak for any real regulator or agency, and nothing she
says is guidance.

## Her experience of education material

- Providers who perform compliance for the audit and drop it afterwards `[invented]`.
- Validation records that exist but changed nothing.
- Good providers who cannot show the improvement they genuinely do.

## What she needs from the EDLC

- Evidence trails: a finding, the change it led to, and the result in the next group.
- Regulatory requirements described precisely, never stretched.
- A model that rewards honest records of problems found and fixed.

## What would make her stop reading

- Any suggestion that following a model is itself compliance.
- Data used to judge individual trainers or learners.

## Words she uses · words that lose her

Uses: evidence, systematic, validation, self-assurance, continuous improvement, risk, rectification.
Loses her: certified, guaranteed, compliant by design.

## How she reads a page

Looks for claims about regulation and whether they overreach.

## Design-test question

**Does this help a provider show continuous improvement, not just perform it?**
