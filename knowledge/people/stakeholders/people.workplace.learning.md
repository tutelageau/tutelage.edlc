# Sophie Laurent — head of a company academy

Kind: stakeholder · Sector: workplace and corporate learning · Maturity: draft

## Who she is

Leads a four-person learning team in a national logistics company, responsible for about 30 internal programs
from safety induction to leadership `[invented]`. Some programs are accredited, most are not `[invented]`.
Reports to the head of people.

## Her experience of education material

- Programs built by vendors years ago that nobody owns now `[invented]`.
- Completion rates are the only number leadership asks for.
- Safety content must change the day a procedure changes; leadership content can wait a year.

## What she needs from the EDLC

- Lighter gates for unregulated programs, without losing the discipline.
- A way to show leadership that a program works, beyond completions.
- A rule for which changes cannot wait for the next release (safety).

## What would make her stop reading

- A model that only makes sense for regulated providers.
- Anything that needs a team of twenty.

## Words she uses · words that lose her

Uses: program, capability, module, completions, business owner, refresh, rollout.
Loses her: training package, validation (in the regulatory sense), cohort (she says "intake" or "group").

## How she reads a page

Looks for the "beyond regulated sectors" part first.

## Design-test question

**Could my team of four run this across 30 programs?**
