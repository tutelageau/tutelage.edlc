# Assoc. Prof. Catherine Lam — associate dean, learning and teaching

Kind: stakeholder · Sector: higher education · Maturity: draft · Layer: 2 Department

## Who she is

Associate dean (learning and teaching) of a faculty of health sciences, three years into a five-year term
`[invented]`. A physiotherapist and researcher by background; born in Hong Kong, raised in Perth `[invented]`.
Chairs the faculty's teaching and learning committee and signs off course proposals before they go to the
academic board `[invented]`.

## Her year with education material

- Reading course proposals and change requests, checking learning outcomes, assessment and the AQF level
  `[GK; S-AQF-2013 lead]`.
- Running periodic course reviews, said to fall at least every seven years `[GK; S-TEQSA-GN-AMRI lead]`, and
  making sure the actions are followed up, not filed.
- Writing papers for the academic board that show the faculty meets the Higher Education Standards Framework
  (`S-HESF-2021`; currency: `S-HESF-AMEND-2026` lead).
- Balancing professional accreditation bodies, whose cycles never line up with the university's `[GK]`.

## What she needs from the EDLC

- A way to see a course's health between reviews, not only every seven years.
- Evidence of improvement she can put in a board paper without six weeks of chasing.
- Something that respects academic judgement and the committee structure, not a parallel process.

## What would make her stop reading

- Anything that adds a committee or a form to a system that already has too many.
- Claims of effect the model cannot back. She reviews research for a living.
- A model built only for vocational providers and relabelled.

## Words she uses · words that lose her

Uses: course, unit, course learning outcomes, course approval, periodic review, academic board, accreditation,
assurance of learning, the HESF.
Loses her: release, pipeline, stakeholder sign-off, competency.

## How she reads a page

Goes straight to the evidence and the governance. If neither is there, she closes it.

## Design-test question

**Would this give my committee better evidence at the next course review, for less effort than now?**
