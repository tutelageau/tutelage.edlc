# Lena Kowalski — secondary science teacher

Kind: stakeholder · Sector: K-12 schooling · Maturity: draft · Layer: 3 Team, 4 Project

## Who she is

Teaches science to Years 7 to 10 and chemistry to Year 11 at a large co-educational state high school, twelve
years in `[invented]`. Grew up in Wollongong; her parents came from Poland `[invented]`. Sits in a science faculty
of nine teachers who share units of work and mark each other's samples `[invented]`.

## Her week with education material

- Writing and rewriting units of work against the state syllabus, which sits on the Australian Curriculum
  (`S-AC-PLAN`, `S-NESA`). Teachers develop the teaching and learning programs; the curriculum does not
  (`S-AC-PLAN`).
- Setting assessment tasks and marking them against achievement standards (`S-AC-LA`).
- Moderating samples with colleagues so a B in her class means a B in the room next door `[GK]`.
- Finding last year's unit in a shared drive with four copies called "FINAL" `[invented]`.

## What she needs from the EDLC

- A way to know which version of a unit was taught, and what changed after the moderation meeting.
- Changes from reflection actually reaching next year's unit, not living in her head.
- Something that fits a term: ten weeks, interrupted by excursions, sport carnivals and relief lessons.

## What would make her stop reading

- Anything that sounds like a new compliance form from the department.
- A model that assumes a design team. Her "team" is three tired teachers in a staffroom at 3.30.
- Words from VET (units of competency, RTO) with no translation.

## Words she uses · words that lose her

Uses: unit of work, program, scope and sequence, assessment task, marking criteria, moderation, the syllabus,
outcomes, faculty, term.
Loses her: cohort release, artefact, stakeholder, benchmark (she says "the standard" or "the syllabus").

## How she reads a page

Skims the first paragraph on her phone during a free period, then asks whether her head teacher would want it.

## Design-test question

**Would this help me hand over a unit to a new teacher without a two-hour meeting?**
