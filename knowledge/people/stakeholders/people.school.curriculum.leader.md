# Daniel Tran — deputy principal, curriculum

Kind: stakeholder · Sector: K-12 schooling · Maturity: draft · Layer: 1 Organisation, 2 Department

## Who he is

Deputy principal responsible for teaching and learning at a Catholic Years 7 to 12 college of about 1,100
students in outer Melbourne `[invented]`. Former mathematics teacher and head of faculty `[invented]`. Son of
Vietnamese refugees; first in his family to go to university `[invented]`.

## His year with education material

- Owning the whole-school scope and sequence across eight learning areas (`S-AC-LA`), and checking faculties
  cover the Victorian Curriculum as the state and the sector authority expect (`S-VCAA-VC`).
- Rolling out a new curriculum version on the state's timeline, which does not match the national one
  (`S-ACARA-CURR`).
- Reporting to parents twice a year in the form the state sets (`S-AC-PLAN`).
- Chasing heads of faculty for updated programs before the sector's review visit `[invented]`.

## What he needs from the EDLC

- One picture of what every faculty is changing, and why, without reading forty units of work.
- A way to show the school board that curriculum review happens on a cycle, not in a panic.
- Room for each faculty to work its own way inside a shared rhythm.

## What would make him stop reading

- A model that ignores the state and the sector authority. They set his calendar.
- Anything that needs software the school would have to buy.
- Jargon he would have to translate before showing it to his heads of faculty.

## Words he uses · words that lose him

Uses: scope and sequence, learning area, faculty, program, curriculum implementation, reporting, the sector,
review, leadership team.
Loses him: release, pipeline, project (he says "unit" or "course"), provider.

## How he reads a page

Prints it, reads it on the train, and writes "so what for us?" in the margin.

## Design-test question

**Could I run our curriculum review cycle on this without adding a meeting to anyone's week?**
