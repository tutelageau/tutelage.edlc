# Jess Okoro — potter teaching online

Kind: stakeholder · Sector: independent, self-directed courses · Maturity: draft · Layer: 1 Organisation, 4 Project

## Who she is

Studio potter for 18 years; for the last four she has sold online courses on wheel-throwing to about 3,000
students worldwide `[invented]`. No formal teaching qualification `[invented]`. Builds every course alone.

## Her experience of education material

- She knows her craft cold, but not how to structure a course; she learned by trial and error.
- Students ask the same questions in comments, which tells her where a lesson fails `[invented]`.
- No standard to work from; she decides what "able to centre clay" means herself.

## What she needs from the EDLC

- A way to write her own "standard" without it feeling like bureaucracy.
- The idea of checking whether students can actually do the thing, not just watch the video.
- Permission to keep it light.

## What would make her stop reading

- Regulators, audits and compliance on every page.
- A model that assumes an institution, a committee or a team.

## Words she uses · words that lose her

Uses: course, lesson, students, the skill, practice, feedback, the next version.
Loses her: learning standard, outcome descriptor, mapping matrix, gate.

## How she reads a page

Looks for an example of someone like her. If there is none, decides it is not for her.

## Design-test question

**Is this for someone like me, or only for institutions?**
