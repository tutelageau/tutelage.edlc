# Layers of scope, by sector · 2026-10-23

Stage 1 (Discover) for the four-layer view (ED-11). Researcher read on 2026-10-23. Source ids refer to
`knowledge/sources/sources.md`.

**Evidence strength.** K-12 is well supported, higher education partly. VET and soft skills rest mostly on leads,
because federal sites (TEQSA, DEWR, ASQA, AQF) timed out. HESF clauses beyond the headings, the 7-year review and the
Core Skills for Work wording need a browser or PDF pass before anything here reaches the site.

## Findings

1. **A layer above the organisation is needed.** In every regulated sector the benchmark comes from outside the
   provider, and in K-12 that layer is two or three deep. Without it the model is false for VET, higher
   education and schools.
2. **"Project" is ambiguous.** In VET the unit of competency is developed but the qualification is released. In
   higher education the unit sits inside a course that is accredited as a whole.
3. **Layers collapse.** Small providers, small schools and independent experts have no separate team or
   department. The model should let layers merge, never require all of them.
4. **Soft-skills benchmarks are adopted, not imposed.** A unit of competency is an endorsed standard a learner is
   assessed against. ACSF, Core Skills for Work and SFIA describe development across levels and are used to
   describe and benchmark. Core Skills for Work reportedly says it is "not a set of standards nor an assessment
   tool" (lead).
5. **Currency warning.** TEQSA pages reportedly mention a HESF amendment in July 2026. Cite the compiled text, not
   the 2021 as-made version, once checked.

## Disagreement carried

The researcher holds that "pillar" is corporate vocabulary (against ED-01's spirit) and proposes
**system · provider · faculty or department · program team · unit**. The case for keeping four layers: the
owner's frame is about scope of ownership inside a provider, and the system layer can be modelled as an input (the
benchmark) rather than a layer. That fits workplace learning, where benchmarks are optional, but fits VET and
higher education less well. Both go to H-7.
