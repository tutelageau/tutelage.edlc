# Starter repository: what goes in, what the rules ask for · 2026-10-23

Stage 1 (Discover) for the starter repository (ED-12). Researcher read on 2026-10-23. Source ids refer to
`knowledge/sources/sources.md`.

## Getting units from training.gov.au

- **Web services** (`S-TGA-WS`, verified): SOAP services at version 13 (Organisation, TrainingComponent,
  Classification). Production access needs credentials from the training.gov.au help desk. The sandbox has a shared
  account, whose details are never committed here.
- **Unit PDFs** (verified): every unit and its Assessment Requirements are published at predictable URLs with no
  login, for example `training.gov.au/TrainingComponentFiles/TAE/TAEASS412_R1.pdf`.
- **REST** (`S-DEWR-NTR-REST`, lead): the department says it is moving the services from SOAP to REST. No
  OpenAPI description or switch-off date found.
- **Licence of unit text** (`S-TGA-COPY`, lead): search snippets say CC BY-ND 3.0 AU. If so, a public repository
  may quote unit text word for word with attribution, but a reworded criterion is a derivative. **Owner decision
  needed before the repository's design is fixed (H-8).**

## The shape of a unit

Worked example: TAEASS412 *Assess competence*, Release 1, TAE v5.0 (`S-TAEASS412-2024`).

- **Unit:** modification history, application, unit sector, six elements with 28 performance criteria
  (1.1 to 6.2), foundation skills, unit mapping information, links.
- **Assessment Requirements:** performance evidence ("at least 6 assessments, at least 1 of which must use
  recognition of prior learning"), knowledge evidence, assessment conditions.
- Range of conditions and prerequisites are absent in this unit, so a data shape must treat them as optional.
- Older units (TAE v2.0, 2022) lay out foundation skills and mapping differently. A reader of unit files must handle
  both.
- The 2025 Training Package Organising Framework (`S-TPOF-2025`, lead) changes templates for new work from
  1 July 2025. Not yet read.

## What the 2025 Outcome Standards ask of the artefacts

From the as-made text of F2025L00354 (`S-NVR-2025`, verified):

- **Assessment tools** are defined as "the instrument, instructions and methods used to gather and interpret
  assessment evidence", including the context and conditions, the tasks, an outline of the evidence, the criteria
  used to judge competency, and the administration, recording and reporting requirements. This is the legal
  skeleton of an assessment tool.
- **1.3(2)(b)** tools are "reviewed prior to use"; **1.3(2)(c)** the review's outcomes inform changes.
- **1.4** principles of assessment (fairness, flexibility, validity, reliability) and rules of evidence (validity,
  sufficiency, authenticity, currency).
- **1.5(2)(b)** each training product validated "at least once every five years", and more often when risks,
  changes or feedback arise; **(c)** risk decides components and sample size; **(f)** the outcome is "not solely
  determined by a person who has designed or delivered the training or assessment"; **(g)** outcomes inform changes.
- **1.1(2)(c)** training "structured and paced to support VET students to progress, providing sufficient time for
  instruction, practice, feedback and assessment".

## The artefacts named in ED-12

| Artefact | What a primary source says |
| --- | --- |
| Assessment tool | Defined in the 2025 instrument (above). TAEASS412 knowledge evidence lists instructions, context and conditions, tasks, "mapping against units of competency". TAEASS502 adds trialling, feedback and version control |
| Mapping matrix | Not defined as a separate document by any source opened. TAEASS412 treats mapping as **part of the tool** |
| Session plan | TAEDES412: sequence, learning activities and a duration per component; facilitation techniques; reviewed against the unit's skill and knowledge requirements |
| Learner guide | No regulator text names it. Nearest: TAEDES502 *Design and develop learning resources* (brief, outline or prototype, content specification, review before release). An open question |

## Machine-readable standards to align to

- **1EdTech CASE v1.1** (`S-1EDTECH-CASE-2025`, verified): CFDocument → CFItem, linked by CFAssociation, a GUID per
  item, REST/JSON. Unit → element → performance criterion maps onto it directly. The best candidate.
- **ACARA Machine-readable Australian Curriculum v9.0** (`S-ACARA-MRAC`, verified), CC BY 4.0
  (`S-ACARA-TERMS`): proves a school-sector benchmark already exists as data, under terms that allow derivatives.
- **ESCO v1.2.1** (`S-ESCO-1.2.1`) for transferable and soft skills; **CTDL-ASN** (`S-CE-CTDL`) as an alternative.

## Disagreement carried

The researcher holds that "mapping matrix" and "learner guide" are customary rather than defined artefacts. They
would model mapping inside the assessment tool and call the guide a "learning resource" until a source says
otherwise. The stage table already calls the mapping matrix stage 3's artefact (paper §5), so this goes to H-8 as
a naming question, not a change to the paper.
