# How lifecycle frameworks tell their story · 2026-10-23

Stage 1 (Discover) for the story-led redesign (ED-09). Researcher read, text-only fetches, 13 of 17 sites read in
full on 2026-10-23. Software frameworks are studied for **storytelling and usability only** (ED-01).

Limit: a text fetch cannot see image maps or interactive diagrams. The behaviour of SAFe's clickable tiles, DORA's
Core diagram and SAMM's grid needs a browser check before we design on them.

## Patterns to borrow

1. **A short canonical text**, versioned, citable and licensed (Scrum Guide, Agile Manifesto).
2. **A numbered spine, rule first**, with previous and next (12factor).
3. **One template for every concept page** (DORA), rendered from data.
4. **The picture is the navigation** (CAST grid, SAFe tiles), with a text equivalent.
5. **Each item states the problem it prevents** (SAFe).
6. **A self-check in a minute that stores nothing** (DORA), with a fuller self-assessment behind it (SAMM).
7. **Maturity as one sentence per level** (SAMM).
8. **Scale as add-on layers or "adjust to your size"** (SAFe configurations, Shape Up).
9. **Routes in by reader and by sector** (ABC LD, CAST, Quality Matters).
10. **A guiding question on each page** (CAST).
11. **Worked stories, a glossary, a "how to begin"** (Shape Up).
12. **Evidence shown openly**, one line per finding (DORA, CAST).

## Anti-patterns

Core content behind a login or a paywall; selling crowding out teaching; navigation deeper than the content;
diagrams with no text version; flat catalogues with no map back and pages with no way on.

## Disagreement carried

The researcher argues against SAFe as the model for showing layers: stacked images, gated articles, the most
commercial site surveyed. CAST's grid and Shape Up's "Adjust to Your Size" handle scale more honestly. Whether the
EDLC has "configurations" at all is a model decision for the paper, not a design choice. It goes to H-7.
