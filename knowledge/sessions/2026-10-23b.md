## Blockers, both cases intact

- **Trainer, design-test "no" (P-42).** "Hold changes to assessment until the next release": Monday's class is
  this release, so nothing he logs helps it. *The model's case:* a cohort should not have its assessment changed
  mid-course except to fix a defect. *Open:* is a marking guide that contradicts its task an accuracy defect?
- **Course coordinator, design-test "no" (P-44).** A committee meets once a year; stages 8 to 10 do not show
  where that year goes. *The model's case:* gates differ in strictness by sector (principle 8). *Open:* the
  higher education sector page and §9 claim 6.
- **"What few institutions yet do" (P-43).** A school teacher and a coordinator read it as false: they review
  constantly; what they lack is the link back to design. *The paper's case:* the operate half is about versions,
  signals and triage, which few do. The owner's text; not yet a memo.
- **Personal email in the first two commits (P-30).** Public and permanent without a force-push, which the contract
  forbids. Future commits use the noreply address (ED-18).
