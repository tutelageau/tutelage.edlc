## The gate checklist

[The gate checklist](/resources/gate-checklist/) is a list of questions to bring to a review, and you can print it today. It lists every stage's exit gate, what the stage produces, and the questions to ask before passing the gate.

It is generated from the same stage data as every stage page, so it never drifts from them. The gates and what each stage produces come from the paper's stage table (§5); "At the gate, ask" is this site's own guidance.

You can take it into a client's review as a list of questions. It is not a compliance tool and does not replace pre-use review or post-use validation. Ticking every question does not make an assessment tool compliant with any standard.

## Sources

Every fact about the world on this site is meant to trace to a row in the [sources register]({{ site.repo }}/blob/main/knowledge/sources/sources.md), kept in the open on GitHub. Each row carries one of three statuses:

- **verified**: opened, read, and the cited detail (author, year, title, clause or page) confirmed against the original;
- **link-checked**: the page opened, but the cited detail is not yet confirmed;
- **from-memory**: nobody here has opened it. Some rows are marked **lead**: a pointer worth following that has not loaded or been read.

A page publishes a claim only when its source is verified. The paper is the one exception. While it carries its draft note, it is published with some references still unverified, and verifying them runs as a tracked job in the register.

At 23 October 2026, eight of the paper's references are recorded from memory: Beck 2002; Brown 1992; Bryk and others 2015; Collins 1992; Cunningham 1992; Forsgren, Humble and Kim 2018; Gentner 1983; Kim and others 2016. Four are link-checked only: Allen 2012; Branson and others 1975; IEEE ICICLE; Molenda 2003. The register, not this page, is current.
