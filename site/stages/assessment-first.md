Assessment first is the core of the model: design the assessments before the learning content. Learning materials then exist to produce outcomes a learner can demonstrate, not the other way round. This is backward design applied as a working rule.

A realistic simulated workplace or case study gives every assessment a consistent, plausible context. The same organisation, people and documents can carry every task in the program.
