Map plans how every outcome will be assessed and delivered, before anything is written. Its defining artefact is the mapping matrix: the record that links each outcome descriptor to the assessment tasks and delivery sessions that address it.

This is the cheapest point in the lifecycle to find errors. Reviewing a coverage plan costs far less than rewriting a finished assessment.

The delivery sessions planned here are provisional. They are fitted to the calendar at [Plan delivery](/stages/plan-delivery/).
