Specify turns the learning standard into structured data rather than a document. Decompose it into outcomes, required knowledge, required skills, evidence requirements, assessment conditions and grading levels. Every later stage traces to these parts.

The same structure can represent a regulated vocational unit, a university subject, or a course authored by an independent expert. Where no external standard exists, this is the stage where you author one.
